## Town

## Creation

Your game takes place in a small town that you and the other players create at the beginning of your session. Your town could be anywhere, but it’s best if it’s relatively isolated - by the sea, in the mountains, deep in the woods or the corn, miles and miles away from anything else.

Most games of Fear of the Unknown are set in something that appears to be the real world in the 20th or 21st century, but that doesn’t have to be the case for your game. There have been games of Fear of the Unknown that took place in 1692 Salem, Massachusetts, or a border town in Cold War Germany, the year 2130 on a colony spaceship, or even a fantasy realm unrelated to the Earth.

You will all collaboratively create the town. Since you all do this together, before the Oracle comes up with the mystery or the other players come up with the protagonists, you’ll all go into those processes with a solid, agreed upon foundation to improvise on top of. This will make it easier for you to make characters and mysteries that go well together.

You should aim for unanimous agreement on the answers to all of these questions. This is a good time to get used to playing and improvising and working with each other.

Town tags are wild tags that players or the Oracle can invoke in scenes that involve them. You can also mark them off instead of marking off an option on Face Peril - sacrificing something in town to save yourself.
When you’re done with creating your town, you’ll have 5 town tags. They’ll represent a location, an NPC, a group of NPCs, a town obsession, and one more thing.
These don’t necessarily represent everything, or even the most important things, in town. Rather, they represent the parts of the town that are most likely to impact the story. You’ll have access to these tags to create scenes out of, getting a +1 on roll you make to Investigate or Face Peril where you invoke them and work them into the story. The Oracle will also be able to invoke them to give you a -1, when they come up with a way for this part of the town to get in your way.

After each question, there's a d66 table of examples . A d66 table uses two six sided dice to generate a random example: roll the first d6 to select one of the groups of 6 examples, and then a second d6 to select a specific example. These are just examples, not an exhaustive list. You are encouraged to make up your own tags!  If  you  can't  come  up  with  one,  the  tables  are  there  as  a backup or for inspiration.

## Name Ask the players to name the town

Everyone should write down the town name. It isn't a tag, but it's useful to remember the name of the place you live. If they can't come up with a name, name it after a local geographical feature - Cliffside or Portsmouth or Lake City, for instance.

## Rumours

Ask each player whether or not they've heard any rumours recently, and if so, what they've heard.

These rumours aren't necessarily true, they're just things that their character has heard. It's up to you, the Oracle, to decide whether they're true or not.
