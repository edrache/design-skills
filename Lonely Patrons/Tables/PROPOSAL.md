# PROPOSAL

Roll 2D6 + relationship bonus.

Relationship bonus: cordial (+0), kind (+1), excellent (+2), super special (+3).

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 7- | They make excuses or don't find it appropriate. |
| 8-10 | They accept if the interaction or proposal seems reasonable. |
| 11+ | They accept without reservation and with pleasure. |
