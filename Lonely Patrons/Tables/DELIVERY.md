# DELIVERY

Roll 2D6 + enhancement bonus:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 2-4 | It doesn't fully convince them, but they keep it... |
| 5-7 | They appreciate it and say they'll play it soon. |
| 8-9 | They find it interesting and are eager to try it. |
| 10 | Their smile reflects great enthusiasm. |
| 11 | It touches their heart, and they thank you profusely. |
| 12+ | They are moved to tears and give you a heartfelt hug. They can't wait to play it. |
