# WIZARD

*You went to the College. You learned to see the Winds of Magic and let one of them move through you. You know what the others at your College whispered: this kind of knowledge changes people. You just didn't know yet in which direction.*

**Damage Die:** d4
**HP:** 4 + CON

## Background

Pick one.

---

**Bright Order Alumnus**

You were trained in the Bright College — Fire, Aqshy, the Red Wind. When you call fire or heat against a target, your spells set things alight on a hit, dealing ongoing burn damage until the fire is extinguished. You cannot cast Ice or Death magic without risk of Miscast.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has seen what happens when I lose control?
- Which of you knows what I left behind in Altdorf?
- Which of you is afraid of fire for reasons that have nothing to do with me?
- Which of you did I test with flame once, and they passed?

---

**Celestial Wayfinder**

You were trained in the Celestial College — Stars, Azyr, the Blue Wind. When you study the night sky, a star chart, or the patterns of a region, roll +INT. On a 10+, the GM tells you one thing about what is coming in this area — a threat, a turning point, or an opportunity — that no one else would know. On a 7-9, you get an impression but not certainty.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you have I seen in my charts before I met them in person?
- Which of you is not supposed to be here, and I haven't decided whether to say so?
- Which of you does not want to know what I saw about their future?
- Which of you has changed something I thought was fixed?

---

**Jade Druid**

You were trained in the Jade College — Life, Ghyran, the Green Wind. When you touch someone who is injured and spend a few minutes in quiet focus, they recover 1d8 HP. When you are in natural terrain — forests, fields, rivers — you feel the Green Wind strongly and take +1 to all moves.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you came to me when the healers were out of answers?
- Which of you thinks what I do is just hedge witchery with a College degree?
- Which of you have I seen damaged in a way that my Wind cannot reach?
- Which of you does the Green Wind warn me about, and I haven't decided what that means yet?

---

**Grey Scholar**

You were trained in the Grey College — Shadows, Ulgu, the Grey Wind. When you are in shadow, fog, or darkness, you can move without being detected — no roll needed unless magic is involved. When you read a person for hidden intent, roll +INT. On a 10+, they cannot hide anything from you with words alone.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you did I follow without them knowing, before we formally met?
- Which of you knows I am not always where I appear to be?
- Which of you has spoken freely in a room they thought was empty?
- Which of you has seen me step out of a shadow and is still not used to it?

## Starting Moves

**Channel the Wind**
When you draw on your Wind of Magic to cast a spell, roll +INT. On a 10+, the spell works as intended. On a 7-9, the spell works but the GM chooses one: the effect is weakened, it is reduced in scope, or you draw dangerous attention from something that senses magic.

**Miscast Warning**
When you push your magic beyond your training — casting twice in quick succession, using a forbidden combination, or reaching for a Wind not your own — roll +INT. On a 7-9, the miscast is minor. On a 6-, something goes wrong that no one present will enjoy.

**Arcane Knowledge**
When you study a magical item, creature, or phenomenon and consult your training, roll +INT. On a 10+, ask three questions about it from the standard list. On a 7-9, ask one, and the GM may ask something of you in return.

**Ward**
When you spend a few minutes inscribing a ward on a location, person, or object, it becomes resistant to one type of harm you specify: fire, cold, Chaos taint, undeath, or similar. The ward lasts until it is triggered or dispelled.

**Read the Winds**
You can sense when magic has been cast nearby, even if you did not witness it. The GM will always tell you when magical residue is present and roughly what kind of power was involved.

**Scholarly Network**
In any Imperial city with a College presence, you can find a fellow wizard. Roll +INT when you reach out. On a 10+, they help freely. On a 7-9, they help but there are strings — academic politics, past debts, or an uncomfortable question they want answered first.

## Advances

- **Mastered Wind:** Your signature Wind magic no longer risks Miscast on a 7-9 result.
- **Counterspell:** When you identify an ongoing magical effect, you may attempt to dispel it by rolling +INT. On a 10+, it ends. On a 7-9, it fades but not without a backlash.
- **Greater Ward:** Your wards can now protect against multiple damage types simultaneously.
- **Arcane Sight:** You can always see active magical effects, enchanted items, and Chaos taint without rolling, even in darkness.
- **Power Tap:** Once per scene, you may enhance a spell without risk of Miscast by spending 1d6 HP.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +2.
- **Superior Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat, at least one other advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +3.

---

## Drives

- Use magic to solve a problem that brute force cannot reach.
- Understand something forbidden that should stay forbidden.
- Protect the people around you from the cost of your power.
- Keep the Winds in balance when someone else is tearing them apart.
