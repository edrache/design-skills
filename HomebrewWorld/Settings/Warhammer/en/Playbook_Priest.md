# PRIEST

*You preach the word of your god and tend to the spiritual needs of the masses. You bless, heal, instruct, and condemn — in the name of a deity that genuinely listens.*

**Damage Die:** d6
**HP:** 6 + CON

---

**Human:** Albrecht, Caspar, Friedrich, Gottwald, Heinrich, Manfred, Oswin, Ruprecht / Adelheid, Brigitte, Hildegard, Johanna, Kunigunde, Mathilde, Ursula, Walburga

**Deity:** Sigmar (patron of the Empire) | Shallya (mercy, healing) | Ulric (war, wolves, winter) | Morr (death, dreams, graveyards) | Manann (seas, storms) | Ranald (luck, thieves, travelers) | Verena (justice, knowledge) | Taal (wild nature, forests)

## Look

Pick one from each row:

**People:** Imperial Human *(only this option)*
**Bearing:** calm authority that never needs to raise its voice | blazing conviction that makes others feel small | weary mercy from someone who has seen too much | cool priestly certainty that god is on your side
**Eyes:** full of compassion even when you condemn | stern and judging | surprisingly cheerful for someone in such a calling | unsettlingly penetrating
**Build:** worn thin by fasting and prayer | surprisingly robust under the robes | upright and noble as a temple pillar | heavy with years of borne sorrows and gear
**Clothing:** clean ceremonial robes with divine symbols | travel-stained vestments | robes combined with plate armour | a habit plain as a vow of poverty
**Signature:** a relic you always keep close | a scar from a trial of faith | a book you open before answering anyone | a quiet prayer murmured in the hardest moments

## Background

Pick one.

---

**Temple Priest**

Your life is ordered by the temple bell and the schedule of services. You have superiors, duties, and faithful who come for a blessing rather than a solution. When you invoke the authority of your temple before the faithful or the hierarchy, you are always heard — the question is what they want from you in return.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you once prayed in my temple — for what, and did you get it?
- Which of you knows my presence here is neither accidental nor entirely voluntary?
- Which of you has something to do with why the temple sent me?
- Which of you owes me a favor because I didn't tell everything I saw?

---

**Wandering Preacher**

You don't wait for the faithful to come to you — you go to those who have never seen a temple. Villages, roads, poverty, and fear — that is your flock. When you arrive at a new settlement or community and speak to those assembled in your deity's name, you always find someone who wants to listen — and someone who would rather you left.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has met me on the road before — and perhaps prefers not to remember the circumstances?
- Which of you comes from a place where my word has already reached?
- Which of you believes in something I told people to abandon?
- Which of you knows I'm preaching as a penance or to pay a debt?

---

**Warrior Priest**

Your deity doesn't want hymns — it wants action. Sigmar demands the sword, Ulric demands strength, Taal demands blood. You became a priest because your god chose you, not because you asked. When you fight in your deity's name against its enemies (Chaos cultists, the undead, servants of Destruction), your attacks deal +1 damage for that fight.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has fought at my side — and knows what I say quietly when I fight?
- Which of you has a problem with my methods but doesn't question the results?
- Which of you knows what I left behind before god called me?
- Which of you is counting on my mercy if things go too far?

## Starting Moves

**Blessing**
You call your deity's grace down on a person, place, or object. Roll +CHA. On a 10+ the blessing holds and has a clear, concrete effect — pick one: protection against what your deity opposes (Shallya — disease; Morr — undead; Ulric — fear); +1 to one type of roll for the scene; or quick healing for 1d6 HP. On a 7-9 the blessing takes hold, but the god wants something in return — the GM says what.

**Prayer in Need**
When you ask your god to intervene in a desperate situation, roll +WIS. On a 10+ the god answers and something changes — you receive a sign, a moment's reprieve, or the breakthrough you needed. On a 7-9 an answer comes, but not necessarily the one you wanted — and it costs you a confession or a sacrifice.

**Theological Knowledge**
When you identify a cult, symbol, ritual, supernatural creature, or spiritual threat, the GM tells you what it is and what its weakness is. You may ask follow-up questions by rolling +INT: on a 10+ ask three, on a 7-9 ask one.

**Spiritual Care**
When you take time to listen to someone who is broken, afraid, or mired in despair, roll +CHA. On a 10+ remove one negative mental condition (fear, shame, madness) or restore someone's will to act — tell me whatever you need for the scene. On a 7-9 relief comes, but the person opens up to you in a way that complicates things.

**Religious Authority**
When you command, warn, or persuade someone who respects your deity or fears divine wrath, your words carry weight. You don't need to roll to make ordinary faithful comply — only to compel someone who truly doesn't want to.

## Advances

- **Invocation:** Once per scene you may call your deity's name as a shield. When you or someone next to you is struck, you may roll +WIS — on a 10+ the hit is negated or deflected, on a 7-9 it is reduced by half.
- **Consecrated Weapon:** You bless a weapon (yours or a companion's) — it becomes effective against the undead, daemons, and servants of Chaos. The effect lasts until the end of the day or until the first fight.
- **Relic:** You acquire or uncover a holy relic of your deity. When you hold it and roll Blessing or Prayer in Need, treat results of 7-9 as 10+.
- **Exorcism:** When you stand before a possessed creature, the undead, or a servant of Chaos and speak the rite of exorcism, roll +WIS. On a 10+ the creature is expelled or destroyed. On a 7-9 it is weakened or forced to retreat, but the rite costs you something precious.
- **Hierarchy:** Your standing in the church grows. You can call on temple resources — shelter, information, subordinate clerics — once per location, if a chapter of your cult exists there.
- **Rousing Zeal:** When you address a crowd or group in a moment of crisis, roll +CHA. On a 10+ all who hear act in accordance with your words for the scene. On a 7-9 half of them do — the GM decides who.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +2.
- **Greater Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat and at least one other Advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +3.

## Drives

- Find proof that your deity truly hears — or that it is you who hears things, and not the god.
- Cleanse a place defiled by a Chaos cult or forces of Destruction before someone worse than you does it.
- Repay a debt to the temple or superior who sent you into the world rather than simply casting you out.
- Find a faithful whose belief is more genuine than your own — and decide what to do about it.
