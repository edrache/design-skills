## Background

Choose one.

---

**Honest Watchman**

You are a rarity — a Watchman who genuinely believes in what he does. You don't take bribes. You don't look the other way. This earns you enemies on both sides of the law. When you expose corruption or stand up for someone who can't pay, the GM must tell you who noticed — and whether they are an ally or a new threat.

When you introduce yourself to the other Player Characters, ask one or more of the following questions:
- Which of you saw me refuse a bribe — and still can't quite believe it?
- Which of you used my honesty to your advantage, knowing I wouldn't lie?
- Which of you tried to bribe me and got a door in the face?
- Which of you understands why I do this — even though they think I'm a fool?

---

**Old Hand**

You know the streets, the rules, and every way around them. You know who to pay, who to ignore, and how to look busy while doing nothing. The system is rotten — you just work within what exists. When you need to turn a blind eye to something convenient, everyone involved understands without a word. Once per session you can avoid the consequences of one action that violates Watch regulations — as long as no one important is watching.

When you introduce yourself to the other Player Characters, ask one or more of the following questions:
- Which of you knows I overlooked something in the past — and it still hangs over my head?
- Which of you benefited from me looking the other way?
- Which of you thinks I'm cynical, but counts on my help when things get hard?
- Which of you knows where I live — and that should worry me?

---

**New Recruit**

You just started your service. The uniform is tight, boots are chafing, you know the regulations by heart — but you don't yet know how it really works. You don't know yet what you've stepped into. When you first witness something that shatters your image of the Watch — corruption, violence from fellow guards, the injustice of the system — roll +WIS. On a 10+ you choose how you react, and it costs you nothing. On a 7-9 you react on instinct and the GM will tell you what consequences follow.

When you introduce yourself to the other Player Characters, ask one or more of the following questions:
- Which of you saw in my eyes the moment I understood everything?
- Which of you tried to warn me — before I joined the Watch?
- Which of you wants to pull me out of this swamp?
- Which of you is counting on me staying exactly as I am?

## Starting Moves

**Authority of the Badge**
When you use your uniform and badge to enforce an order, restore order, or intimidate someone who won't listen, roll +CHA. On a 10+ you do it without a scene and without witnesses who will remember. On a 7-9 it works, but the GM chooses one: someone remembers this | you lose something minor | someone goes to fetch your superior.

**Know Your Beat**
You know how your district smells when something's wrong. When you enter territory you have patrolled before, the GM will tell you: what has changed since you were last here, who is lurking without good reason, and where someone could be hidden or ambushed. If you want to know more, roll +INT; on a 10+ ask three questions, on a 7-9 ask one.

**Street Fighting**
You don't fight sportingly — you fight to win and to stay standing. When you fight in a tight space, a crowd, or a dark alley, deal +1d4 damage. When you subdue someone instead of killing them, the roll succeeds on a 7+.

**Network of Informants**
You have your people — the cheese seller who sees everything from the corner; the kid who stares down from the rooftop; the innkeeper who never sleeps. When you look for information about someone or something in the city, roll +CHA. On a 10+ you get what you want with no strings attached. On a 7-9 you get it, but one of your people wants something in return — now or later.

**Patrol**
When you spend a night on a quiet patrol of your district, choose two:
- You learn who in the area is doing something illegal
- You notice someone in need before it's too late
- Your superior hears you were active — and is pleased
- You avoid something unpleasant that was waiting for you elsewhere

## Advances

- **Interrogation:** When you speak with someone in an official Watch context — and you hold any advantage over them — roll +CHA. On a 10+ choose three truths that will come to light. On a 7-9 choose one, but you also show some of your cards.
- **Old Enemy:** Choose one gang, faction, or criminal group in the city. You know more about them than anyone. You always know where to find them and how to recognise them — and they know who you are.
- **Street Tough:** When you take a hit that could put you down, roll +CON. On a 10+ you stay on your feet with 1 HP. On a 7-9 you go down, but you do it in a way that doesn't reveal your weakness.
- **Back-Alley Lawyer:** You know the regulations well enough to bend them. When you want to detain, search, or arrest someone on dubious grounds, the GM must give a specific reason why it fails — or it succeeds.
- **Natural Sergeant:** When you lead a group into action (even just two Player Characters), roll +CHA instead of +DEX when coordinating. On a 10+ everyone acts as one. On a 7-9 it works, but one person does it their own way.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, up to a maximum of +2.
- **Higher Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat and at least one other Advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, up to a maximum of +3.

## Drives

- Prove that at least one piece of the system can work honestly.
- Find evidence of your superior's corruption — and decide what to do with it.
- Protect someone the system already wrote off.
- Live to retire with your bones intact and a clear conscience — or at least one of the two.
