## Background

Pick one.

---

**Templar of Sigmar**

You carry a Sigmarite writ of authority signed in Altdorf. When you formally invoke your authority to imperial citizens, officials, or soldiers, they cannot legally refuse your demands for aid, information, or access — though they may delay, misdirect, or quietly warn your targets.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has seen me burn someone who deserved it?
- Which of you thinks I have gone too far at least once?
- Which of you knows what I am looking for on this road?
- Which of you helped me once and never asked what I found?

---

**Confessor's Shadow**

You were trained to extract truth from those who do not wish to give it. When you interrogate a prisoner or examine someone for signs of corruption, roll +WIS. On a 10+, you learn what you need to know, and you may choose whether the subject remains cooperative afterward. On a 7-9, you learn the truth, but something — evidence, a relationship, an innocent's dignity — is damaged in the process.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you knows someone I have questioned before?
- Which of you has sat on the other side of my methods?
- Which of you thinks I take too much satisfaction in this work?
- Which of you trusts my judgment less than my results?

---

**Heretic Hunter**

You have catalogued the signs, sigils, and rituals of every Chaos cult operating in the Empire. When you examine a crime scene, a body, or a strange event for evidence of cult activity, the GM tells you: which cult, what they were doing, and what they left behind.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has killed a cultist before I could find them?
- Which of you has something in their possession I have seen in cult hands before?
- Which of you knows I am already on a trail?
- Which of you has been mistaken for a cultist by someone like me?

---

**Survivor of Corruption**

Someone close to you fell to Chaos, and you did what had to be done. You are immune to the fear and revulsion others feel when confronting mutation and Chaos taint — you have already seen the worst of it on a face you loved. When you act to protect others from Chaos taint at personal cost, mark XP.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you knows what I lost?
- Which of you lost someone too, and chose a different path?
- Which of you has seen me hesitate when it mattered?
- Which of you is watching me for signs that the taint found me too?

## Starting Moves

**Witch Sense**
When you study a person, place, or object for signs of Chaos corruption, roll +WIS. On a 10+, the GM tells you clearly: is it tainted, how deeply, and what power left the mark. On a 7-9, you get a strong impression — tainted or clean — but the details are incomplete or deeply unsettling.

**Confess**
When you confront someone with evidence of their sin and demand the truth, roll +CHA. On a 10+, they tell you what you need to know. On a 7-9, they tell you something true — but not everything, and they now fear or hate you for asking.

**Pyre's Edge**
When you attack a confirmed servant of Chaos — a cultist, mutant, daemonhost, or corruption-bearer — deal your damage and take +1 forward against them for the rest of the scene.

**Unbreakable Will**
When you face magical compulsion, daemonic terror, or Chaos-spawned psychological assault, take +1 to resist. On a 12+, you are immune to that specific source for the remainder of the encounter.

**Read a Crowd**
When you observe a gathering of people for hidden guilt, loyalty, or cult activity, roll +WIS. On a 10+, pick two: who is afraid, who is lying, who is a leader, or who is marking you. On a 7-9, pick one.

**Silver and Fire**
Your weapons deal full damage to daemons, the undead, and Chaos-tainted creatures regardless of mundane defenses they might possess.

## Advances

- **Burning Conviction:** When you deal damage to a servant of Chaos, you may add your WIS modifier to the damage.
- **Network of Informants:** In any imperial settlement, you can spend time to make contact with local informants. They know one secret about the powerful, the corrupt, or the hidden.
- **Torturer's Knowledge:** When you use Confess on someone who is physically restrained, you always get the full truth, no roll needed — but the GM may have consequences follow you.
- **Daemon-Ward:** You carry or wear a sigil blessed by a Sigmarite priest. Daemons and summoned entities suffer -1 ongoing against you.
- **No Mercy:** When you kill a confirmed servant of Chaos, you may immediately make a free attack against a second target in reach.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +2.
- **Superior Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat, at least one other advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +3.

## Drives

- Expose a cult before it can complete its ritual.
- Burn something that should not have been allowed to live.
- Protect the innocent from a threat they cannot see.
- Face your own potential for sin and choose the harder path.
