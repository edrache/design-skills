# RAT CATCHER

*With the help of your faithful and ferocious dog companion, you save civilization from the terrible rat plague.*

**Damage Dice:** d6
**HP:** 8 + CON

## Background

Choose one.

---

**City Rat Catcher**

You know every street, cellar, and midden in the city like your own pocket. You know where rats nest, who pays per tail, and who hires you because they won't dare go down to their own basement. When you search for someone or something hidden in urban back-alleys — cellars, slum districts, gutters — you always know where to start. The GM tells you what else is there.

When you introduce yourself to the other Player Characters, ask one or more of the following questions:
- Which of you has seen me at work and would rather forget it?
- Which of you has used my services without asking for details?
- Which of you knows who I owe a favour to in this city?
- Which of you thinks my work is simple — and is wrong?

---

**Sewer Warden**

You go where others refuse. The sewer network is your home — you know the underground labyrinth better than the streets above. When you descend underground — into sewers, catacombs, tunnels, or dungeons — roll +WIS. On 10+ choose three, on 7-9 choose one: you know which way to go; you know what lives down here; you know where the exit is; you sense an ambush before it reaches you.

When you introduce yourself to the other Player Characters, ask one or more of the following questions:
- Which of you came down into the sewers with me and saw something down there?
- Which of you knows that something beneath the city is looking for me?
- Which of you helped me get out alive from somewhere I shouldn't have entered?
- Which of you suspects I know more about what's under the city than I'm letting on?

---

**Skaven Hunter**

You've seen too much. You know that some of the "too large" or "too clever" rats are something else entirely — something the authorities prefer not to know about. You carry this knowledge and understand what it means: you are dangerous to someone far more threatening than an ordinary rodent. When you identify signs of Skaven activity or their presence, you always correctly recognise them. The GM tells you how old the signs are and what they suggest.

When you introduce yourself to the other Player Characters, ask one or more of the following questions:
- Which of you knows about the Skaven? How is that possible?
- Which of you wants me to reveal what I know — even if it gets us killed?
- Which of you thinks I'm paranoid — until they see it themselves?
- Which of you has already had the chance to run before they understood what from?

## Starting Moves

**My Dog**
A faithful, ferocious dog travels with you — small but unstoppable. Give it a name. The dog attacks on your command (damage d6, *vicious* tag), searches terrain, and never fails when tracking rats. When your dog is threatened or injured, you may throw yourself in front of it — you take the hit instead. When your dog dies, you lose access to all moves that rely on it until you obtain a new one and train it — this takes at least a week.

**Poisons and Bait**
You know how to prepare lethal bait for rodents — and it works on other creatures too. When you prepare a poison or poisoned bait and have the appropriate ingredients, roll +INT. On 10+ your poison is effective and will act without delay. On 7-9 it works, but the GM chooses one: it takes longer than you planned; someone else might stumble upon the bait; the ingredients are used up and you can't repeat the process.

**Setting Traps**
When you have time and materials, you can set a trap for any creature. Roll +DEX. On 10+ the trap works perfectly — choose two effects. On 7-9 choose one: the target is immobilised; the target is stunned; the target deals -1d4 damage for a round; the trap is difficult to detect. You may also leave traps in rooms — the GM will tell you when someone triggers one.

**Fighting in Tight Spaces**
In narrow corridors, sewers, and cellars, you are in your element. When you fight in a confined space or tunnel, your melee attacks deal +1d4 damage and enemies cannot overwhelm you with numbers — treat them as a single opponent regardless of how many there are.

**Hardened**
Years of working in sewers and contact with rodents, filth, and rot have toughened you like few others. You are resistant to disease: when exposed to plague, poison, or infection, roll +CON. On 10+ the effect is completely blocked. On 7-9 the effect is significantly reduced. Additionally: you never lose your nerve at the sight of rats, decay, or ordinary foulness — this is your daily life.

## Advances

- **Pack of Dogs:** You may have up to three dogs at once. Each additional dog works like your main dog, but is less trained — once per scene you may send a dog to search an area or attack without rolling.
- **Sewer Rat:** You move through sewers and underground spaces at normal walking speed even in complete darkness. You never lose your bearings underground.
- **Fearless (Rats):** You are completely immune to panic caused by rats, rodent swarms, and lower-ranking Skaven. When others must roll for fear, you don't.
- **Network of Contacts:** In every city you have a former client, ingredient supplier, or guild colleague. When you arrive somewhere new, roll +CHA. On 10+ you have someone useful here — the GM tells you who and how they can help. On 7-9 you have someone, but they owe you something or want something from you.
- **Master Trapper:** You can now set traps unnoticed even while under observation. On 10+ when setting a trap, choose three effects instead of two.
- **Skaven Sense:** When Skaven are nearby — within a few dozen metres — you instinctively feel it. The GM will always inform you when Skaven presence is detectable.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +2.
- **Higher Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat and at least one other Advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +3.
