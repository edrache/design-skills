## Background

Pick one.

---

**Guild Thief**

You were trained by a thieves' guild — pick a city. You know the signs, passwords, and protocols. When you enter a settlement, you can find the local guild contact without rolling. They will give you work, safe house access, and information about local marks — for a cut.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has something I already appraised while we were talking?
- Which of you knows the same guild I do, but from the other side?
- Which of you has already noticed I took something?
- Which of you owes the same people I owe?

---

**Street Informant**

You have contacts among the city's forgotten: beggars, dock workers, sewer cleaners, street children. When you ask around about someone or something in a settlement, roll +CHA. On a 10+, your network delivers accurate information freely. On a 7-9, the information is real but partial, or the contact needs a favor first.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has asked me for information before and didn't ask where I got it?
- Which of you knows someone who knows me, and doesn't know it?
- Which of you have I been asked to report on, and have declined so far?
- Which of you knows a part of this city I have never been able to map?

---

**Fence and Dealer**

You know how goods move. When you want to sell something — stolen, unusual, or legally ambiguous — roll +CHA. On a 10+, you find a buyer, get fair value, and no awkward questions are asked. On a 7-9, you find a buyer, but they want to know its provenance, or they offer significantly below value.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has sold me something without knowing what it was worth?
- Which of you is carrying something I recognize from a job list?
- Which of you am I currently working for, without them knowing?
- Which of you trusts my appraisal more than their own judgment?

---

**Escaped Debtor**

Someone important wants something from you — money, a favor, or information — and you are running. When you are in a settlement, you can sense when someone connected to your debt is near, without rolling. You always know when to leave before they see you.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you knew me before I had this problem?
- Which of you knows the shape of it without knowing the details?
- Which of you has covered for me at least once?
- Which of you do I worry will be used against me?

## Starting Moves

**Pickpocket**
When you attempt to take something from someone without them noticing, roll +DEX. On a 10+, you have it and they don't know. On a 7-9, you have it, but choose one: they notice something's wrong, they're watching you now, or you'll need to move quickly.

**Dark Alley**
When you attack from hiding or close range without warning, roll +DEX. On a 10+, deal your damage and you remain hidden or can flee before retaliation. On a 7-9, deal your damage but you are exposed.

**Read the Mark**
When you observe someone and assess what they're worth — financially, informationally, or as leverage — roll +WIS. On a 10+, ask two: What do they value most? What are they hiding? What would make them cooperate? What is their weakness? On a 7-9, ask one.

**Blending In**
When you adopt a disguise, role, or false identity, roll +CHA. On a 10+, you are convincing for the scene unless you do something to break it. On a 7-9, you're convincing until someone looks closely or asks the wrong question.

**Citywise**
In any city or large town you have visited before, you always know where to find: a black market, a fence, a safe house, or a corrupt official. No roll needed.

**Second Exit**
When you enter a building, room, or enclosed space, ask the GM: where's the other way out? They tell you. It's always there.

## Advances

- **Master Pickpocket:** You never roll for Pickpocket in crowds or when the mark is distracted.
- **The Guild Owes Me:** Once per session, call in a favor from a criminal organization. They provide muscle, information, passage, or a distraction — nothing that gets them killed.
- **Knife Work:** When you deal damage with a small weapon (dagger, knife, hidden blade), deal +1d4 damage.
- **Con Artist:** When you tell a deliberate lie and roll +CHA, a 7+ always gets you out of immediate trouble. The long-term consequences are the GM's to invent.
- **Urban Predator:** When you track a person through a city, you never lose them unless magic is involved.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +2.
- **Superior Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat, at least one other advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +3.

## Drives

- Get paid more than the job was worth.
- Find something that gives you leverage over someone powerful.
- Get out of a situation that should have been impossible to escape.
- Protect someone small from someone large, for a change.
