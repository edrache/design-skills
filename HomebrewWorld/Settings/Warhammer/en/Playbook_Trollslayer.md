# TROLLSLAYER

*You have done something that cannot be undone. You swore the Oath, shaved your head and dyed what remains, and now you seek a death worthy of the shame you carry. Something big. Something that takes you with it.*

**Damage Die:** d10
**HP:** 8 + CON

---

## Name

Pick a name:

**Dwarf (only):** Gotrek, Ungrim, Borin, Drengi / Helga, Dagni, Berta, Skarla

## Look

Pick one from each row:

**People:** Dwarf — there is no other ancestry for a Slayer
**Bearing:** bellowing and alive | quietly furious | oddly calm about all of it | talking to death like it owes you a favor
**Eyes:** bright with the joy of ending | flat and far away | searching for something worthy | weeping without knowing it
**Build:** short and impossibly broad | knotted like old rope | scarred from crown to boot | vibrating with barely contained violence
**Crest:** the orange crest is freshly waxed | the orange crest is scorched at the edges | the orange crest has something braided into it you don't talk about | the crest is older than it looks
**Marks:** tattoos cover every visible surface | only the oath-scar, plain and deep | enough iron rings to weigh down a lesser being | a clan-sigil burned over, unrecognizable now

## Background

Pick one.

---

**Oathbreaker**

You broke a vow you made before your ancestors. The specifics are yours alone. What matters is: the shame cannot be unmade, and neither can you. When you charge into combat against a creature bigger or more dangerous than yourself and sustain a wound in doing so, mark XP.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you knows what I did?
- Which of you asked and was told to mind their own?
- Which of you thinks you could have done better in my place?
- Which of you has seen me almost die and seemed relieved I didn't?

---

**Kinslayer**

Your shame is worse than most. You do not speak of it. When you fight alongside an ally who is in true danger — unconscious, outnumbered, or about to die — you may throw yourself between them and the harm without a roll. You take the hit. No questions. No hesitation.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you knows enough to never ask?
- Which of you saved my life without understanding why that makes things worse?
- Which of you treats me with a pity I find intolerable?
- Which of you looked me in the eye and said nothing, and I respected that?

---

**Dragonslayer Aspirant**

You have already killed things most adepts never survive: trolls, giants, daemons of modest power. When you identify a creature as your next worthy death — bigger, badder, or more dangerous than anything you have faced before — take +1 forward on all moves against it for the rest of the session.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has watched me pick my target and known there was no stopping me?
- Which of you thinks I will find my worthy death before this journey ends?
- Which of you is quietly hoping I don't?
- Which of you has killed something alongside me and knows it felt wrong that we both walked away?

---

**Elder Slayer**

You have been walking this road long enough that younger Slayers have heard of you. Your name carries weight in Slayer lodges. When you walk into a Slayer lodge or announce your deeds to other dwarfs, they treat you as something between legend and warning. Ask the GM what they know of you here.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has heard my name somewhere else before we met?
- Which of you thinks I am a cautionary tale rather than an inspiration?
- Which of you wonders why I am still alive after this long?
- Which of you is the reason I'm still here, whether they know it or not?

---

## Starting Moves

**Death Wish**
When you charge into combat outnumbered or against a creature larger than yourself, deal your damage and take +1 armor against the first attack that hits you. You may not flee from this fight without dishonor.

**Born to End Things**
When you deal the killing blow to a creature of significant size or power, immediately recover 1d6 HP.

**Grudge Bearer**
When something or someone wrongs you or your companions, name it. You deal +1d6 damage against that creature or person for the rest of the session. You may only carry one active Grudge at a time.

**Unbreakable**
When you would be knocked unconscious or killed by a single blow, roll +CON. On a 10+, you take the damage but stay standing — barely. On a 7-9, you go down but do not die; you may act one final time before losing consciousness.

**Old Scars**
Your body is a map of every fight you have survived. When someone tries to intimidate or manipulate you through threats of bodily harm, it does not work. At all.

**Troll Killer**
You know exactly how trolls work. When you fight a troll, stone troll, river troll, or similar regenerating creature, your attacks always prevent regeneration, and you deal +1d4 damage on a hit.

## Advances

- **Daemon Slayer Reputation:** When you enter combat against a daemonic entity, all other Chaos creatures in the area must roll or flee.
- **Nothing Left to Lose:** Once per session, when you would die, you don't. The GM decides the cost — broken bone, lost gear, a terrible wound — but you live. This time.
- **Massive Blow:** Once per combat, when you hit with a melee weapon, you may choose to deal maximum damage instead of rolling.
- **Endure:** When you are poisoned, diseased, or otherwise physically afflicted, treat the condition as one severity lower than it is.
- **Worthy Death Magnetism:** At the start of each session, declare one creature type as your nemesis. The GM will ensure that creature type appears at some point this session.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +2.
- **Superior Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat, at least one other advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +3.

---

## Drives

- Die fighting something that deserves to kill you.
- Protect someone who cannot protect themselves, even though you're not supposed to care.
- Find something big enough to be worthy of the Oath.
- Honor the Oath by never, ever running.
