# ROAD WARDEN

*The roads don't guard themselves. Between settlements is where the Empire thins out — where bandits set up toll-points, where mutants come down from the hills, where something from the forest has been taking livestock and is working its way up in ambition. You know what lives out there. You're still going out there anyway.*

## Background

Pick one.

---

**Veteran Patrol**

You have ridden this stretch of road for years. When you approach any settlement, inn, toll road, or crossroads in the Empire, ask the GM one: who has passed through recently, what is the local danger right now, or who is watching this place.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you have I stopped and questioned before?
- Which of you has information I have been trying to find?
- Which of you travels these roads more than they should?
- Which of you knows someone I am still looking for?

---

**Survivor of the Road**

You have walked away from an ambush, a Beastman raid, or worse — and you know now what survival requires. When you are ambushed or caught in a trap, you are never surprised. You act in the first round regardless.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you was there when it happened?
- Which of you knows the story and has never brought it up?
- Which of you is still making the mistake I made that day?
- Which of you I'd trust to have my back if it happened again?

---

**Bandit Network**

You have cultivated informants among the criminal elements of the roads — former outlaws, toll-point operators who look the other way, roadside innkeepers with flexible loyalties. When you look for information on criminal activity along a road, roll +CHA. On a 10+, you have a contact here and they talk freely. On a 7-9, you have a contact but they want something first.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you is on a list I keep inside my coat?
- Which of you has used one of my contacts for their own ends?
- Which of you thinks my methods are too permissive?
- Which of you have I let go with a warning once?

---

**Beast Tracker**

You have learned to read what comes down from the forests — Beastmen, mutants, Chaos-touched wildlife — from the marks they leave on the land. When you study a trail, carcass, or disturbed site for signs of creature activity, the GM tells you: what made it, how many, how recently, and which direction they went.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has followed me into the dark and lived?
- Which of you trusts my read of the land even when I don't explain it?
- Which of you has seen me lose a trail and knows I have been looking ever since?
- Which of you carries something that came from the forest that I don't think was found there?

## Starting Moves

**Know the Road**
When you travel between two settlements on a road you have patrolled, you arrive without incident unless you choose to investigate something along the way. You know the roads, the shortcuts, and where the dangers sit.

**Patrol Strike**
When you attack with a road weapon — sword, lance, crossbow — from horseback or from a prepared position, deal your damage and add +1d4 if your target did not know you were there.

**Imperial Warrant**
When you invoke your authority as a Road Warden to stop, search, or detain someone on imperial roads, roll +CHA. On a 10+, they comply without trouble. On a 7-9, they comply but are hostile, slow, or have already hidden what you're looking for.

**Hard Miles**
You do not get lost on imperial roads, even in darkness, heavy fog, or after an ambush. When you guide a group through road travel under difficult conditions, they arrive safely.

**Read the Threat**
When you observe a group of people on the road and assess them for danger, roll +WIS. On a 10+, ask three questions from the standard list. On a 7-9, ask one.

**Wilderness Shelter**
When you make camp off-road in the Empire's wild spaces, roll +WIS. On a 10+, you find a safe site and your group recovers as if resting at an inn. On a 7-9, the site is safe but something out there knows you are here.

## Advances

- **Mounted Combat Mastery:** When fighting from horseback, your damage die improves by one step, and your horse acts on your initiative.
- **Road Network:** You have contacts at every major coaching inn between two cities of your choice. They will feed you, shelter you, and pass messages for free.
- **Outlaw Knowledge:** You know the faces, habits, and territories of every active bandit group in one region. The GM will always tell you if you recognize a criminal.
- **First Responder:** When you arrive at a scene of violence or chaos and take charge, all NPCs present treat your orders as legitimate authority.
- **Fortified Position:** When you have time to prepare a defensive position, you and your allies gain +1 armor while defending it.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +2.
- **Superior Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat, at least one other advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a max of +3.

## Drives

- Keep a road safe that someone else gave up on.
- Find what is preying on travelers and end it.
- Protect someone who cannot afford protection.
- Discover what the bandits are actually working for.
