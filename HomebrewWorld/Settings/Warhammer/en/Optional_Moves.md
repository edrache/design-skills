# Optional Moves — Warhammer Fantasy

**Resist the Taint**
When you are exposed to Chaos corruption — through contact, a failed ward, or prolonged time near a daemon or mutant — roll +CON. On a 10+, you resist it cleanly. On a 7-9, you resist, but you carry a trace of it: a small mark, an intrusive thought, or a detail the GM notes quietly. On a 6-, the corruption takes hold; the GM describes what changes and when it will matter.

**Navigate the Crowd**
When you move through a city in haste — chasing, fleeing, or trying to reach someone before they vanish — roll +DEX. On a 10+, you gain on your target or lose your pursuer, your choice, at no cost. On a 7-9, you gain ground but choose one: you knock someone over, lose something in the chase, or end up somewhere unexpected.

**Bribe or Threaten**
When you try to get cooperation from an official, guard, guild member, or bureaucrat who has no reason to help you, roll +CHA. On a 10+, they cooperate fully for a reasonable price or credible threat. On a 7-9, they cooperate, but the cost is higher than expected or the cooperation is partial — and they remember your face.

**Push Through Chaos Territory**
When you travel through land touched by Chaos — corrupted forest, mutant-held roads, the burned edge of a Northman raid — roll +CON. On a 10+, you pass through intact and learn something about the state of the corruption here. On a 7-9, you pass through but the journey costs something: supplies, morale, or a wound that is harder to explain than it looks.

**Find What the Empire Buries**
When you dig into the history of a location, family, institution, or event and ask who was really responsible, roll +INT. On a 10+, you find it. The GM tells you where the buried record, hidden witness, or suppressed evidence is — and who would not want you to have found it. On a 7-9, you find a thread that leads somewhere, but following it will cost time, risk, or a favor owed.
