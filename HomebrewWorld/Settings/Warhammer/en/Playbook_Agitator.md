# AGITATOR

*You are charismatic and quick with words. You fight for your cause against the official institutions — and you know that between revolution and the gallows there is only one bad day.*

**Damage Die:** d6
**HP:** 6 + CON

## Background

Pick one.

---

**Street Pamphleteer**

Words are your weapon. You write, print, and scatter leaflets — at markets, near churches, on city gates, before the watch figures out where they came from. When you put your pamphlet into someone's hands or read it aloud to a crowd, the GM tells you who took notice — both ally and enemy.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you helped me hide after the watch was hunting for the printshop?
- Which of you read something of mine and never admitted it?
- Which of you knows where I get my paper and ink?
- Which of you thinks my words are dangerous to someone they care about?

---

**Religious Agitator**

You speak in the name of faith — but not the official, convenient kind that suits the powerful. You collect donations, rouse the fervent, and preach that the clergy has betrayed its calling. When you address a crowd in the name of religious values (justice, mercy, truth), roll +CHA. On a 10+ at least one person in the crowd actively supports you and is ready to act. On a 7-9 you move them, but someone runs for a priest or a guard.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you heard my sermon and changed their mind about something important?
- Which of you thinks I go too far in the name of faith?
- Which of you holds beliefs I am actively challenging?
- Which of you knows I have a hidden patron inside the Church?

---

**Revolutionary Agitator**

You don't write — you organize. Strikes, marches, collective refusals to pay taxes. Your name is on a list in more than one office. When you organize ordinary people into collective action (protest, strike, escape, resistance), you always find a few who are ready — the question is what they will demand of you in return.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you was with me the last time everything went wrong?
- Which of you knows who turned me in before?
- Which of you owes me their freedom or their livelihood?
- Which of you is afraid I'll drag them into something there's no coming back from?

## Starting Moves

**Rousing Speech**
When you address a crowd publicly or try to convince a group of people to your cause, roll +CHA. On a 10+ the crowd is with you — the GM says what they are prepared to do. On a 7-9 you stoke their enthusiasm, but they want something immediate before the fire dies down.

**Network of Contacts**
In every city of the Empire you know someone — a printer, an innkeeper, a preacher, an official with a conscience. When you seek shelter, information, or aid among ordinary people, you always find someone willing to help. The GM will say what they want from you.

**Word Against Steel**
When you are stopped by guards, officials, or soldiers and no violence has occurred yet, roll +CHA. On a 10+ you talk your way out without consequences — maybe you challenge their authority, maybe you cite the law. On a 7-9 you buy time or get through, but the GM says what you leave behind.

**Provoke and Run**
When you deliberately provoke someone powerful (an official, a noble, a priest) in front of witnesses, roll +INT. On a 10+ you expose them to ridicule or public anger and escape unscathed. On a 7-9 the provocation lands, but you must leave something behind or sacrifice someone in the escape.

**Read the Crowd**
When you observe a gathering of people or listen in on street conversation, the GM tells you the mood: who has grievances, what they fear, and who is ready to act. You may use this in your next social roll.

## Advances

- **Demagogue:** When you succeed at a Rousing Speech, the crowd does not merely act — your words stay with them. The GM will say what lasting belief or commitment they carry away.
- **Pamphleteer with a Press:** You own or have reliable access to a printing press. You can mass-produce leaflets, posters, and calls to action — enough to shift opinion in a district or a city.
- **Helpers:** Three dedicated pamphleteers work with you in the field — distributing leaflets, watching for the watch, creating diversions on command. Treat them as hirelings from the Core Rules.
- **Hidden Patron:** You gain a powerful ally — a merchant, a noble, or a high-ranking priest — who funds your operation in exchange for favors. The GM will create this figure and determine what they want.
- **Smooth Words:** When you lie, embellish the truth, or play on emotions in a direct conversation, roll +CHA. On a 10+ the other person believes you and acts accordingly. On a 7-9 they believe you but want confirmation from someone else.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +2.
- **Greater Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat and at least one other Advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +3.

---

## Drives

- Bring down someone who harms the weak and walks the world unpunished.
- Build a movement that will outlast you — no matter what happens to you.
- Find out who turned you in — and decide what you will do about it.
- Find the balance between the cause and the people the cause is destroying.
