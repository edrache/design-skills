# BEGGAR

*You're a sharp one, making your living on the generosity of others — and the gift of persuasion. The streets know your name. The wealthy don't even see your face.*

**Damage Die:** d4
**HP:** 6 + CON

## Background

Pick one.

---

**Invisible Observer**

You've sat on the same corner for years. The wealthy look through you like air — and so they say things in your presence they'd never say near anyone who mattered. When you observe a place or person for a time without making contact, the GM tells you one secret or fact not commonly known — and asks if you want to know more.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has seen me on the corner and not said good morning in months?
- Which of you knows I heard something they thought no one heard?
- Which of you feels vaguely guilty for ignoring me but can't say why?
- Which of you spoke to me once — and remembers it more than I do?

---

**Misery Actor**

Begging is a craft. You can look more wretched than you are, summon pity on command, and blend into any crowd as someone else entirely. You have access to a basic disguise kit. When you play someone from the lower rungs of society or fake an infirmity or illness, you always get what you came for — the question is whether anyone is watching too carefully.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has seen me in two different faces and is only now connecting them?
- Which of you has used my acting talents — officially or otherwise?
- Which of you thinks I know too much about their weaknesses?
- Which of you gave me a coin before they knew who I was?

---

**King of the Streets**

You know every beggar, drifter, and night-wanderer in the area. You command respect — or at least deference — in circles the wealthy consider invisible. When you need someone to watch a place, hide something, carry a message, or vanish into a crowd, your beggar network does it for free — or for a very small favor.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you heard my name spoken with respect at the market, without knowing who I was?
- Which of you once needed discreet help and received it without asking how?
- Which of you knows there are dozens of ears behind me?
- Which of you likes to think they're on the right side — and is grateful to me for it?

## Starting Moves

**Invisible**
When you enter a place where your presence is socially expected (a market, a gate, outside a tavern), you are treated as invisible. Guards don't see you. Wealthier people look through you. You can approach close, listen, and observe without a roll — unless you do something that draws attention.

**Beggar's Wisdom**
The streets are the best intelligence service in the city. When you ask your beggar network about a person, place, or event, roll +CHA. On a 10+ you get three useful facts: one about the past, one about the present, one about what this person fears. On a 7-9 you get two pieces of information but must pay for them with a favor.

**Survival**
You've lived in gutters, slept in frost, and eaten things others wouldn't touch with a stick. When exposed to harsh conditions — cold, hunger, disease, exhaustion — roll +CON. On a 10+ ignore the effects entirely. On a 7-9 you survive, but pay a small price: a scar, a weakness, bad sleep.

**Act of Desperation**
When you appeal to the pity, mercy, or guilt of someone wealthier than you, roll +CHA. On a 10+ you get what you want — money, help, passage, forgiveness. On a 7-9 you get half or something conditional; the GM says what this person expects in return. Doesn't work on someone who has already seen through you.

**Knowledge of Back Ways**
You know every alley, sewer passage, hidden gap, and hideout in the area where you've begged. When you flee or need to disappear, you always find a way — the question is whether you're dragging someone with you who doesn't know these places.

## Advances

- **Informant Network:** Your beggar network reaches further. You can use Beggar's Wisdom even in an unfamiliar city if you have a day to make contact. On a 10+ you gain a bonus: one of your informants stays on there permanently.
- **Unexpected Strike:** In desperation, you have surprising force. When you attack from hiding or when no one expects you, deal +1d6 damage. Once per scene.
- **False Face:** Your disguise becomes masterful. You can spend an entire scene as someone else — a different race, gender, or profession — without a roll, if you have a moment to prepare.
- **Companion:** A steady companion joins you — another beggar, a loyal dog, or a quick-footed child messenger. Treat them as a hireling from the Core Rules. If they die, it is a serious thing.
- **Hideout:** You have a safe place in the city — a cellar, an abandoned turret, a crawlspace behind a tavern wall. No one finds you there unless you lead them to it.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +2.
- **Greater Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat and at least one other Advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +3.

---

## Drives

- Find something that lets you crawl out of the gutter — for good.
- Discover who is behind everything that has touched your network.
- Repay someone who helped you survive when you were a child.
- Prove — to yourself and others — that a beggar sees more than everyone combined.

- Hidden compartments in rags — can conceal two small items
- Kitchen knife (small, d4 damage)
