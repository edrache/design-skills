## Background

Pick one.

---

**Guild Scholar**

You completed formal training — a laboratory, textbooks, a master at your shoulder. Your knowledge is systematic and certified. When you identify an unknown substance, plant, or preparation, the GM tells you: what it is, how it works, its uses — and one hidden danger most people don't know about.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you once used my services without asking about the price?
- Which of you knows what I'm looking for outside the city?
- Which of you has a health problem I could solve — or complicate?
- Which of you thinks I'm naive until they look at my notes?

---

**Grey Market Dealer**

You know where to buy and who to sell what the Guild officially doesn't offer. Stimulants, hallucinogens, perception-altering compounds — you have access to things it's better not to know about. When you search the black market for a substance, ingredient, or information about a poison, roll +CHA. On a 10+ you find what you want without consequences. On a 7-9 you find it, but someone notices — the GM will say who.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you bought something from me under a false name?
- Which of you knows I owe a favor to someone dangerous?
- Which of you sees me as a route to something hard to obtain?
- Which of you thinks I know too much to live quietly?

---

**Wandering Collector**

Rare ingredients don't grow in cities. You've crossed the Empire's back roads hunting herbs, roots, and substances no shop carries. When you search for a natural alchemical ingredient in the wilderness or forest, you always find it — the question is how long it takes and what else lives there.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you once accompanied me on an ingredient-gathering expedition?
- Which of you knows what I've been hunting for months without finding?
- Which of you saved me — or didn't — when I got into trouble in the field?
- Which of you knows that I came back from one expedition a completely different person?

## Starting Moves

**My Kit**
Your pack contains medicines, dressings, and field preparations. When you treat someone who is not in immediate danger, you restore them to full health. When you treat someone in the field, roll +WIS. On a 10+ restore 1d8 HP. On a 7-9 you heal them for 1d8, but you use up something hard to replace.

**Identify Substance**
When you examine an unknown compound, poison, plant, or alchemical preparation, the GM tells you what it is. For further questions, roll +INT; on a 10+ ask three, on a 7-9 ask one.

**Brew Preparations**
When you have access to the right ingredients and equipment, you can prepare a healing tincture, a sedative, or a contact poison. It takes several hours; the GM will say what you need.

**Medical Knowledge**
When you administer first aid to a dying person or make a diagnosis, you always know what threatens the patient and what could save them — even if the treatment itself requires a roll.

**Poison Resistance**
Years working with dangerous substances have had their effect. When you are exposed to a poison, disease, or consciousness-altering substance, roll +CON to negate the effect entirely. On a 7-9 you reduce the effect by half.

## Advances

- **Master Poisoner:** Poisons you create deal +1d4 additional damage and may carry one special effect: paralysis, disorientation, or unconsciousness.
- **Lab in a Pack:** You can brew preparations in the field without dedicated equipment. It takes the same time, but you don't need a workshop.
- **Valuable Knowledge:** When you sell your products or medical services, you always get more than expected. The GM will tell you what you receive for a standard price.
- **Bookworm:** When you research an academic topic in an available written source, you always find what you're looking for — if anyone ever recorded it.
- **Apprentice:** A young student joins you, following simple orders, carrying equipment, and learning. Treat them as a hireling from the Core Rules.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +2.
- **Greater Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat and at least one other Advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +3.

## Drives

- Discover the ingredient that can cure an incurable disease.
- Prove your methods are better than the quackery everyone accepts as truth.
- Repay a debt to someone who funded your equipment when you had nothing.
- Establish a workshop and reputation great enough that the Guild cannot ignore you.
