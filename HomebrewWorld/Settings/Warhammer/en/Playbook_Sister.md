# SISTER

*You dedicate your life to your deity, taking vows of lifelong service. Your strength comes from stillness, not from rank.*

> "Sister" is a conventional name — this playbook works equally well for brothers, monks, and friars. Play this character regardless of gender if this path speaks to you.

**Damage Die:** d6
**HP:** 6 + CON

---

## Name

Pick a name:

**Feminine:** Agatha, Hildegard, Josephine, Clara, Constance, Leokadia, Matilda, Seraphina
**Masculine:** Ambrose, Benedict, Celestine, Donat, Hilary, Modest, Onuphrius, Simeon

## Look

Pick one from each row:

**People:** Imperial Human *(the only available people)*
**Bearing:** focused and silent | composed even in chaos | warm but with limits you do not cross | weary, yet unbending
**Hands:** rough and cracked from work | delicate, ink-stained | strong from years of physical labor | healing hands — familiar with every herb and bandage
**Build:** lean as fasting and prayer | sturdy from years of service | straight as monastic rule | bent from a burden carried for a long time
**Clothing:** grey or brown hooded habit | a travel-worn robe stained by the road | no habit — only a holy symbol marks you in the crowd | plain garb with a clear sign of your deity
**Signature:** prayer beads or knotted cord always in hand | monastic ring or seal | tired eyes that see more than they say | the smell of wax and herbs that never quite washes out

## Background

Pick one.

---

**Keeper of the Relic**

Your order entrusted you with something sacred — a relic, a manuscript, an object so old no one remembers its origin. You are not a priest or a warrior. You are a keeper. When you protect someone or something that has been placed in your care, you have advantage on defense rolls — roll twice and take the better result.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has seen what I carry — and still has questions they haven't asked?
- Which of you helped me smuggle my charge through a difficult checkpoint?
- Which of you suspects I know more about what I carry than I'll ever admit?
- Which of you protected me when I could not protect myself?

---

**On Pilgrimage**

Your order sent you on the road — through the Empire's holy sites, to a distant chapel, bearing a blessing for the sick. You have been walking for months. You know the roads, the inns, local cults, and what's best avoided along the way. When you guide the group through unfamiliar terrain or seek shelter in a strange place, you always know where it's safe to stop — the question is what fortune will charge for it.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you met me before — on the road, in a tavern, around a fire?
- Which of you knows where I'm headed and has opinions about it?
- Which of you joined me because they're looking for something on this road themselves?
- Which of you saved me when I was caught off guard in the field?

---

**Sister with a Sword**

Your order is militant — or the world made you take up arms before your vows were complete. Your faith is no softer for knowing how to fight. You know violence is a last resort, and you know when that last resort has arrived. When you go into battle to defend the innocent, the helpless, or those entrusted to you, you deal +1 damage and ignore the first instance of poison or fear effects in any scene.

When you introduce yourself to the other PCs, ask one or more of the following:
- Which of you has seen me fight — and still has questions?
- Which of you thinks a sister with a sword is a contradiction in terms?
- Which of you needs my protection but won't admit it?
- Which of you was there when I broke my vow of nonviolence — and what do they think about that?

## Starting Moves

**Monastic Vows**
You have taken vows — of abstinence, humility, obedience, or another renunciation. As long as you keep them, you are resistant to fear and mental manipulation — fear effects and magical compulsion act on you with delay or reduced force. If you break a vow, you lose this move until you perform penance or renew your vows at a holy site.

**Deity's Blessing**
When you lay hands on someone wounded or dying and speak a prayer, roll +WIS. On a 10+ restore 1d8 HP and remove one effect of poison or disease. On a 7-9 heal for 1d6, but it costs you something — the GM will say what.

**Monastic Craft**
Your order taught the virtue of working hands. Choose one craft: *brewing*, *herbalism*, or *calligraphy*. When you use your chosen craft, you always know what to do and what you need. The products of your work carry additional value — your ale is more fortifying, your herbs heal more reliably, your calligraphy opens doors closed to the illiterate.

**Knowledge of the Sick**
When you tend to someone ill, wounded, or dying and have at least an hour of quiet, you always know what ails them and how to ease it. Even without the means, you know what to seek. When a diagnosis cannot be made by ordinary means, roll +INT; on a 10+ you learn the cause and one hidden root of it.

**Protect the Flock**
When you place yourself between a threat and someone defenseless — a ward, the wounded, a child, the innocent — the GM cannot deal them damage as long as you live and stand between them. You may take damage in their place; if you do, roll +CON to resist the worst of it.

## Advances

- **Higher Vow:** Your Monastic Vows deepen — you take on one additional vow (choose with the GM). As long as you keep it, you gain +1 to +WIS rolls.
- **Relic:** You carry a relic of your deity. Once per session you may invoke it to automatically pass a fear roll, drive off undead, or enhance Deity's Blessing by +1d6.
- **Monastic Authority:** When you present yourself as a representative of your order, people listen — even if they don't know your deity. You gain +1 to +CHA rolls when dealing with the faithful and common folk of the Empire.
- **Shield of the Community:** You may extend Protect the Flock to the whole group — once per scene you may take damage for any PC within sight.
- **Pure Soul:** You are resistant to magical corruption and Chaos effects — your faith acts as a shield. Roll +WIS when exposed to such effects; on a 10+ you ignore them entirely.
- **Improved Stat:** Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +2.
- **Greater Stat** *(Requires: Improved Stat and at least one other Advance)*: Increase one of your stats by +1, to a maximum of +3.

---

## Drives

- Complete the mission your order entrusted to you — whatever it costs.
- Find a place where your faith can take root and offer shelter to others.
- Prove — to yourself or the world — that stillness and service are stronger than rage and steel.
- Atone for breaking the vow you consider most sacred.

## Equipment

Carry 9.

- Religious symbol — crucifix, amulet, or reliquary (small)
- Monastic habit with hood (0 armor, 1 slot)
- Satchel of healing herbs — bandages, salves, tisanes (1 slot)
- Craft tools (choose one: *herbalist's kit*, *inkwell and parchment*, or *small cask and yeast*) (1 slot)
- Prayer book or monastic rule (small)
- Pilgrim's rope and walking staff (1 slot)
- Modest provisions for a few days (small)
- Purse of alms — not your money, but your order's offerings (small)
