# Advanced World of Dungeons — Level 2 & 3 Abilities

Each entry adds new effects to the existing ability. Higher levels do not replace lower ones — all levels are active simultaneously.

---

## Alchemy
*Level 1: You can imbue magic within a potion, salve, or item. You start with three alchemical formulas.*

**Level 2:** You can combine two effects from the "The user will..." list into a single formula. The preparation cost doubles, but the result delivers both effects at once. Additionally, you can prepare two separate alchemical items in a single session without needing to double the time.

**Level 3:** You can create a Legendary Formula — a single preparation that combines one option from every category plus one exotic effect of your own invention, subject to GM approval. Additionally, any formula you create can now be made permanent: it does not expire after use, but recharges after one full day.

---

## Affluent
*Level 1: When your purse is empty, you can attempt to make purchases on credit.*

**Level 2:** Once per session, you can call in a debt — name an NPC who owes you and the GM will make them come through with coin, goods, or a significant favor. They may not be happy about it, but they deliver.

**Level 3:** Your financial reputation is unassailable. You no longer need to roll when making purchases on credit — it is simply assumed. Once per campaign, you can arrange financing for a truly massive acquisition (a ship, a building, an army's provisions) with no immediate funds required.

---

## Allure
*Level 1: When you seduce someone, you can make them reveal intimate secrets. You can attract someone's attention with only a glance.*

**Level 2:** Those you have previously seduced feel a lingering protectiveness toward you. Once per session, one such NPC will intervene on your behalf at a critical moment without being asked.

**Level 3:** Your allure is overwhelming. Once per session, you can make someone completely devoted to your wellbeing for a full scene — they will not harm you, lie to you, or allow others to harm you without a WIS roll to resist. They remember the experience afterward but cannot explain why they acted that way.

---

## Appraise
*Level 1: You can tell the approximate value of artwork and trade goods after inspecting them.*

**Level 2:** Your eye extends to the magical. When you examine a magical item, the GM must reveal one of its properties or powers to you.

**Level 3:** You can appraise anything — curses, blessings, the value of information, even the worth of a person's life in the GM's economy. Once per session, ask the GM: "What is the most valuable thing in this location?" The GM must answer honestly.

---

## Backstab
*Level 1: Add +3 damage to melee attacks when the target is unaware of your presence.*

**Level 2:** When you Backstab and deal damage, you also choose one: the target is silenced, the target drops what they are holding, or you immediately melt back into the shadows before they can react.

**Level 3:** Your Backstab damage bonus increases to +5. When you Backstab and kill a target in a single strike, no witness immediately realizes what happened — the death appears natural, accidental, or the result of someone else, unless actively and carefully investigated.

---

## Bargain
*Level 1: Once per day, you can halve the price of a purchase through contacts and guild connections.*

**Level 2:** Every successful Bargain doubles as an information exchange. When you close a deal, you may ask the merchant one question about local happenings, rare goods, or notable people nearby.

**Level 3:** You can Bargain for things that are not technically for sale — secrets, favors from powerful figures, dangerous information, even freedom. The GM must engage with your offer rather than dismiss it. Something is always on the table, even if the price is steep.

---

## Berserk
*Level 1: When outnumbered, gain +1 damage and +1 armor. You must resist resorting to violence in heated situations.*

**Level 2:** When your HP drops to half or below while Berserk, your damage bonus increases to +2. Enemies who witness your frenzy must pause before engaging you.

**Level 3:** You cannot be knocked unconscious while Berserk — you fight until your HP reaches a negative value equal to your CON score, at which point you collapse. Additionally, your frenzy is contagious: one ally who can see you fighting gains +1 damage and +1 armor for as long as your Berserk lasts.

---

## Bless
*Level 1: With holy water, anoint items as holy and magical (+3 damage vs. Evil) for a short time.*

**Level 2:** You can Bless a person instead of an object. A blessed person gains +1 armor against Evil and immunity to fear from undead for the rest of the day.

**Level 3:** You can Bless a location rather than a person or item. For 24 hours, the blessed area has the following properties: Evil creatures suffer disadvantage when entering; allies who rest there recover an extra Hit Die; the location cannot be scried or tracked by magical means.

---

## Brawny
*Level 1: +1 melee and thrown weapons damage. Re-roll damage once per attack.*

**Level 2:** When you roll maximum damage on a melee or thrown attack, the target is knocked prone or loses their next action recovering.

**Level 3:** Your melee and thrown damage ignores 1 point of armor (minimum 0, applied after normal armor reduction). When you knock a target prone via maximum damage, you may immediately grab and pin them — they remain pinned until they succeed on a STR roll.

---

## Cloaked
*Level 1: When under the cover of darkness, gain +1 armor.*

**Level 2:** While in darkness, you move silently without a roll, as long as you take no aggressive action. Enemies cannot target you with ranged attacks unless they have supernatural senses.

**Level 3:** Once per day, you can become completely invisible in any lighting condition for up to one hour. You remain invisible even when attacking, until the end of the round in which you struck. The effect ends if you speak loudly or use a light source.

---

## Connected
*Level 1: Attempt to obtain favors from nobles — loans, invitations, introductions.*

**Level 2:** Your network extends into criminal, mercantile, and underworld circles. Once per session, call on a non-noble contact for a significant favor.

**Level 3:** You have a contact at every level of society. Once per session, name any type of person — a spymaster, a pirate captain, a high priest, a crime lord — and you have a contact of that type. They owe you enough to meet with you and genuinely hear your request.

---

## Contort
*Level 1: You can escape from bonds or squeeze into small or tight places.*

**Level 2:** You cannot be held, pinned, or grappled by anything short of magical restraints. You can squeeze through any gap your head fits through.

**Level 3:** Your body endures compression and positions that should be lethal. You can survive being buried, crushed in rubble, or physically trapped indefinitely without harm. You also reduce falling damage by half, regardless of the height.

---

## Cutpurse
*Level 1: When you pick pockets or conceal things on your person, you can do so quickly while running.*

**Level 2:** When you successfully pick a pocket, you gain a mental catalogue of everything the target is carrying — not just what you took.

**Level 3:** You can steal things that are not simply on a person's body — a key actively being turned in a lock, a document being actively read, information someone is clearly thinking about. Nothing a person is actively interacting with is beyond your reach. The GM determines the exact mechanics case by case.

---

## Cure
*Level 1: Neutralize poisons, remove curses, and heal wounds without a touch.*

**Level 2:** Your Cure now works at range — up to a room away. Once per day, you can attempt to Cure a magical disease or terminal illness.

**Level 3:** Your healing is miraculous. Once per day, you can perform a Full Restoration — bringing a character at 0 HP to half their maximum HP, simultaneously cured of all poisons, curses, and diseases. No components required. This works even at the very threshold of death, as long as they have not been dead for more than a minute.

---

## Damnation
*Level 1: Attempt to inflict curses or ailments through eye contact or a fetish.*

**Level 2:** Your curses are tenacious. Targets who attempt to remove your curse must overcome your WIS score in addition to normal removal methods.

**Level 3:** Once per session, you can place a Doom — a named curse with a specific triggering condition of your choosing ("the next time you betray a friend, you will lose your voice for a week"). The Doom activates automatically when met and cannot be removed by normal means — only by undoing whatever act of harm you named.

---

## Daring
*Level 1: Once per day, ignore the damage or effects of an environmental hazard.*

**Level 2:** Your Daring now covers magical hazards and supernatural effects. When you succeed at something others declared impossible, one ally who witnessed it gains a bonus to their next roll.

**Level 3:** Your Daring is unlimited — you can use it any number of times per day. Additionally, when you perform an act of extraordinary courage that others witness, all allies present cannot be affected by fear or doubt for the rest of the scene.

---

## Diplomat
*Level 1: You can negotiate with anyone intelligent enough to understand you.*

**Level 2:** Agreements you broker hold firm — NPCs won't renege unless significantly provoked. You can negotiate with creatures who don't share your language through gesture and tone.

**Level 3:** Once per session, you can declare a Parley — a period of enforced negotiation. All parties must negotiate in good faith for at least one full scene before violence can resume. Even enemies who were attacking moments before must pause. This cannot be invoked if lives are actively being lost in that instant.

---

## Disguise
*Level 1: With costumes and makeup, convincingly imitate others or hide your own identity.*

**Level 2:** You can maintain a disguise indefinitely without effort. You can alter your voice convincingly enough to fool people who know the person you're imitating.

**Level 3:** After studying someone for at least an hour, you can take on their mannerisms, emotional tells, and knowledge of private matters convincingly enough to fool even their closest family and confidants. When disguised as someone specific, you can answer questions about their private life as if you had access to their memories — the GM will fill in what you would plausibly know.

---

## Divination
*Level 1: Gazing into a crystal or holy water, attempt to see distant, past, or future events.*

**Level 2:** Once per day, without any prop, you may ask the GM one yes/no question about the immediate future (within the next hour). The answer is honest, if sometimes cryptic.

**Level 3:** By touching a location or object, you can witness its past as vivid visions. Once per session, you can ask the GM to show you one specific event from the past related to your current investigation — they describe it as if you were present.

---

## Duelist
*Level 1: You treat any one-handed weapon as Light.*

**Level 2:** When wielding a Light weapon with your off-hand empty, add +1 to attack rolls. When an enemy misses you, you may immediately strike back for half damage.

**Level 3:** In declared one-on-one combat, you are essentially untouchable by less skilled opponents. When fighting a single opponent in a formal or clearly declared duel, you have advantage on all rolls and they have disadvantage on all rolls against you.

---

## Engineer
*Level 1: With the Crafting skill, create and repair mechanical devices and firearms.*

**Level 2:** You can jury-rig a working mechanical device from scrap in minutes. Once per session, a device you built functions as an improvised explosive (d6+2 damage, small area).

**Level 3:** Given adequate materials and time, you can construct any mechanical device you can conceptualize — siege engines, automata, traps of extraordinary complexity. Once per session, a contraption you build on the fly works perfectly on the first use, no roll required.

---

## Expert
*Level 1: Once per day, turn a Miss into a Partial Success.*

**Level 2:** You can use Expert twice per day. When you invoke it, a compelling explanation may earn an additional narrative benefit.

**Level 3:** You can use Expert any number of times per day. Additionally, once per session, you can turn a Partial Success into a Full Success — not just a Miss into a Partial.

---

## Familiar
*Level 1: A magically bonded animal companion. You can see through its eyes and hear what it hears.*

**Level 2:** You can speak through your Familiar's mouth. If your Familiar is killed, you can bond with a new one after a day of ritual.

**Level 3:** You can fully inhabit your Familiar's body — your own body falls unconscious while you act through your Familiar completely. You can maintain this for up to one hour. If your Familiar is killed while you inhabit it, you take d6 damage and are stunned for a round.

---

## Full-contact magic
*Level 1: Channel Wizardry spells through your fists in melee. You remain unharmed even if the spell affects the area around you.*

**Level 2:** You can hold a charged spell in your hand for up to one minute, delivering it to the next creature you touch.

**Level 3:** You can now channel any spell — not just Wizardry — through your fists. When you channel a Sorcery spell through a strike, it deals additional damage equal to your STR modifier. Your unarmed strikes are always considered magical weapons.

---

## Gadgetry
*Level 1: Solve risky problems with gadgets, roll +INT. Level 3/6/9 devices replicate Sorcery spells.*

**Level 2:** Your gadgets have two charges before breaking down. Your daily-use Sorcery-replicating devices may be used one additional time per day.

**Level 3:** One of your gadgets becomes a Masterwork — a permanent device that never breaks down and can replicate its assigned Sorcery spell at will, with no daily limit. You choose which of your existing devices receives this status.

---

## Getaway
*Level 1: Once per day, automatically flee a location — sprinting, dodging, ducking.*

**Level 2:** When you use Getaway, you can bring one willing ally with you. You always know the fastest exit from any location you've been in for more than a minute.

**Level 3:** Getaway now works for your entire party simultaneously. Additionally, you can pre-designate a safe house or exit point at the start of any session — when you use Getaway, the party arrives there regardless of the distance between you and it.

---

## Guarded
*Level 1: Once per day, redirect an attack against you to one of your Hirelings.*

**Level 2:** Redirected Hirelings take half damage. You can use Guarded twice per day.

**Level 3:** You can now redirect attacks to yourself instead of a Hireling, absorbing the blow for any ally. When you do, you take only half damage — the rest is mitigated by your reflexive guard. This version of Guarded can be used any number of times per combat.

---

## Hardy
*Level 1: Add +6 to your hit points.*

**Level 2:** Gain an additional +4 HP (total +10 from Hardy). When reduced to 0 HP, roll +CON — on a 7+, you drop to 1 HP instead.

**Level 3:** Gain an additional +6 HP (total +16 from Hardy across all levels). You are immune to instant-death effects and lethal environments. When reduced to 0 HP, you automatically stabilize — no roll required.

---

## Heavy Hands/Feet
*Level 1: 1d6 unarmed damage. +1 damage at levels 3, 5, 7, and 9.*

**Level 2:** Your unarmed strikes are magical — they affect creatures resistant to non-magical damage. Hitting the same target twice in one turn staggers them: they act last next round.

**Level 3:** Your unarmed strikes deal 2d6 damage instead of 1d6 and count as Martial Weapons for all purposes. Once per combat, you can deliver a Devastating Strike — dealing your maximum possible unarmed damage automatically, no roll required.

---

## Hunter
*Level 1: Add +3 damage to ranged attacks when the target is unaware of your presence.*

**Level 2:** When you fire from hiding and hit, you remain hidden — enemies don't immediately locate you. You may fire once more from the same position before needing to move.

**Level 3:** You can maintain concealment indefinitely in any natural environment, never needing to relocate after firing. Any target you hit this combat is Marked — all sources of damage against a Marked target deal +1 additional damage, as you've identified their weak points for your allies.

---

## Illusions
*Level 1: Create visual illusions or mesmerize targets.*

**Level 2:** Your illusions include convincing sound. Targets that fail to see through them remain affected for one round after you stop concentrating.

**Level 3:** Your illusions are fully multi-sensory — sight, sound, smell, temperature, and touch. Any target interacting with your illusions must make a WIS roll to disbelieve them. Particularly convincing illusions can cause real psychosomatic damage (d4, GM's discretion based on the severity and violence of the illusion).

---

## Inspire
*Level 1: Attempt to sway public opinion with song, stories, or rhetoric.*

**Level 2:** You can direct a crowd toward a specific action — not just opinion but behavior. The GM will tell you what it would take to push them too far.

**Level 3:** Once per session, your words create a lasting change — not a momentary reaction. A crowd you've inspired will remember your message for weeks and may act on it independently, long after you've left. The GM should incorporate the consequences into future sessions.

---

## Instinct
*Level 1: Re-roll DEX when reacting to dangerous situations.*

**Level 2:** You can re-roll DEX twice per scene and keep the better result. You are never caught flat-footed by an ambush.

**Level 3:** You have advantage on all DEX-based rolls. Once per session, just before catastrophe strikes, your instincts fire ahead of it — the GM gives you one free action to react before the disaster fully unfolds.

---

## Linguist
*Level 1: You can speak and read all civilized languages.*

**Level 2:** You can decode ancient, extinct, or monstrous languages given enough time. You notice immediately when someone is speaking in cipher or concealed subtext.

**Level 3:** You can understand any language, including those of creatures, animals, spirits, and magical entities, with no prior exposure needed. Given a few minutes of listening to a completely unknown language, you can communicate basic ideas in return.

---

## Lookout
*Level 1: When leading in city or dungeon, always spot targets before they spot you.*

**Level 2:** When you spot an enemy before they spot you, you silently warn all allies — everyone acts first in the ensuing encounter.

**Level 3:** You are never caught unaware. You automatically detect hidden creatures, concealed doors, and people in disguise within your line of sight. The GM must tell you when something is not as it appears, even if you don't know specifically what is wrong.

---

## Lucky
*Level 1: Once per day, turn any Miss into a Success.*

**Level 2:** Once per session, you can use Lucky on behalf of any ally you can see, turning their Miss into a Success.

**Level 3:** You can use Lucky three times per day on yourself and once per session on any ally. When you invoke Lucky, you may also ask the GM for one additional piece of good fortune alongside the mechanical effect — a timely distraction, a misfire, a lucky coincidence that helps your position.

---

## Magic-fueled hit points
*Level 1: Extra HP equal to Power score. Lose 1–2 magic HP per Wizardry spell.*

**Level 2:** When magic-fueled HP are depleted, you can fuel Wizardry spells by spending regular HP at a 2:1 ratio.

**Level 3:** Your magic-fueled HP regenerate during combat — you recover 1 magic HP at the start of each round, up to your Power score maximum. When your magic-fueled HP are at their maximum, your Wizardry spells deal +1 additional damage.

---

## Mind Shield
*Level 1: Immune to fear, charm, and sleep spells.*

**Level 2:** Once per day, you can extend Mind Shield to one nearby ally for a full scene.

**Level 3:** Your mind is an absolute fortress. You are immune to all forms of mental intrusion — scrying, telepathy, possession, and magical compulsion of any kind. When someone attempts to magically affect your mind, you immediately sense the attempt and know the general direction it came from.

---

## Opportunist
*Level 1: When you and your allies outnumber opponents, inflict +2 damage.*

**Level 2:** When you hit an already-engaged foe, they have disadvantage on their next action against anyone other than you.

**Level 3:** Once per round, whenever any ally deals damage to a target, you may immediately make a free attack against that same target. This does not consume your action — it is the reflexive strike of a master tactician reading the flow of battle.

---

## Pacify
*Level 1: Attempt to pacify or charm (demi)intelligent creatures with your voice.*

**Level 2:** Creatures you have Pacified remain favorably disposed toward you for days.

**Level 3:** Once per session, you can Pacify an entire crowd or group simultaneously — not just one creature. The effect lasts for a full scene. Creatures you Pacify at this scale may become genuinely sympathetic to your cause if you speak to them clearly while they are calm.

---

## Perform
*Level 1: Earn d6s per level per day entertaining a crowd. A rolled 1 turns the audience hostile.*

**Level 2:** A rolled 1 now causes minor awkwardness, not hostility. All dice showing 4+ attracts a patron or important admirer.

**Level 3:** One of your performances has become legendary in a specific region — your name alone opens doors, earns favors, and creates trust there without any roll. In new regions, a single memorable performance establishes this reputation permanently. The GM should track where your legend has spread.

---

## Pet
*Level 1: A loyal and effective animal companion.*

**Level 2:** Your companion can follow complex multi-step instructions without you present and return with a signal about what it found.

**Level 3:** Your bond with your companion transcends the mundane. Choose three of the following traits for your companion: can speak one sentence per day in your language; can perform any complex task without instruction; is immune to fear and magical charm; can detect magical auras within 30 feet; can track any target it has met at least once regardless of time elapsed.

---

## Prestige
*Level 1: Everyone knows who you are. Hirelings cost half price.*

**Level 2:** Your name opens doors coin cannot. Once per session, reputation alone grants access to something normally refused. You receive free lodging and meals where you are known.

**Level 3:** Your reputation is so formidable that enemies may choose not to attack you to avoid the notoriety. Once per session, declare your name and deeds to a group of enemies — they may stand down entirely without combat, if doing so doesn't directly contradict their core purpose or orders.

---

## Reflexes
*Level 1: Always act first. Can react when suddenly surprised.*

**Level 2:** Once per combat, when an enemy misses you, you may act immediately out of turn, taking one full action.

**Level 3:** You have advantage on all DEX-based rolls. When you act out of turn via your Level 2 ability, you may take two actions instead of one. You also automatically succeed at dodging any environmental hazard that can be avoided with fast reaction — no roll required.

---

## Rockskin
*Level 1: Rock-hard skin (Armor 2).*

**Level 2:** Bludgeoning damage is reduced by an additional 1 (minimum 0). Immune to minor environmental damage — heat, cold, abrasion.

**Level 3:** Your Rockskin Armor value increases to 3. You are immune to poison delivered through skin contact or small wounds and cannot be affected by bleeding effects. Your skin is hard enough to damage unprotected weapons — light weapons that strike you directly take a notch (GM's discretion on breakage).

---

## Rover
*Level 1: Move through wilderness without leaving obvious tracks or a trail.*

**Level 2:** You can erase your entire party's tracks. You can read others' tracks as a written account — numbers, pace, weight, emotional state.

**Level 3:** You have no natural predators in the wilderness. Animals will not attack you unprovoked, ever. You can survive indefinitely in any natural environment without supplies — you always find water, shelter, and sufficient food regardless of conditions.

---

## Sailor
*Level 1: Crew a sailing vessel. Never feel ill in stormy seas.*

**Level 2:** You can navigate without charts. Once per voyage, your quick action automatically prevents one catastrophic outcome.

**Level 3:** Any vessel you captain operates at its absolute peak. Once per voyage, you automatically succeed at any navigation crisis that would otherwise sink the ship — storm, reef, monster, mutiny — no roll required. Sailors serving under your command never mutiny; they will endure conditions that would break others.

---

## Sapper
*Level 1: When using explosives or artillery, add +1 die to damage and discard the lowest.*

**Level 2:** You can construct improvised charges from available materials in an hour. Your explosives can be set with a ten-minute delay fuse or a tripwire trigger.

**Level 3:** Your explosives are precision instruments. You can create shaped charges that destroy specific structural targets — walls, doors, foundations — without harming adjacent areas or people. Once per session, you can set a catastrophic delayed trap that causes a significant and lasting environmental change: collapses a tunnel, brings down a tower, floods a room.

---

## Schemer
*Level 1: Bribe or blackmail others subtly, through third parties.*

**Level 2:** When a scheme goes wrong, there is always one degree of separation protecting you. An NPC intermediary takes the fall before it reaches you.

**Level 3:** You can set a scheme in motion that plays out over weeks or months without you being present. Once per session, you can declare that a scheme you arranged earlier has come to fruition — the GM must incorporate a meaningful positive outcome from your prior planning into the current scene.

---

## Scout
*Level 1: When leading in the wilderness, always spot targets before they spot you.*

**Level 2:** When you scout and return, you identify two points of interest. Your companions gain advantage on their first roll at each of those locations.

**Level 3:** You cannot be surprised by any environment you have scouted. When you scout an area and return, the GM must reveal all threats, hazards, and points of interest in that area — nothing is withheld. Your scouting reports are always complete.

---

## Shifting
*Level 1: Drink an animal's blood to attempt to take its form.*

**Level 2:** You no longer need blood — one minute of contact with an animal is enough. While shifted, you retain your INT and can form simple plans and speak short, broken sentences.

**Level 3:** You can now shift into any animal you have ever encountered, with no contact or blood required. While shifted, you retain all your mental stats, can speak normally, and may use any non-physical ability you possess. Shifting back is effortless and instantaneous.

---

## Skirmish
*Level 1: +1 damage. Worn armor counts as one type lighter.*

**Level 2:** When you deal damage with Skirmish, you can immediately disengage without triggering any opportunity strikes and re-engage another target.

**Level 3:** You can engage and disengage with any target in a single fluid action with no consequences ever. When you deal damage with Skirmish, choose one additional effect: the target drops their weapon, you steal a small item from them, or you Mark them — your next attack against that target has advantage.

---

## Slay
*Level 1: +2 melee damage.*

**Level 2:** When you reduce an enemy to 0 HP with a melee attack, you may immediately make a second attack against any adjacent enemy.

**Level 3:** Your total melee damage bonus from Slay becomes +4. Once per combat, before rolling to attack, you may declare a Killing Blow — if you hit, the target is reduced to 0 HP regardless of their current HP total. This does not work on named, significant NPCs or boss-level creatures unless the GM deems it dramatically appropriate.

---

## Sleuth
*Level 1: Find clues quickly while moving. Always know when someone is lying.*

**Level 2:** When you detect a lie, you also sense the emotional shape of the deception — fear, greed, loyalty, shame.

**Level 3:** Once per session, when you have gathered enough information, you can declare a Deduction — a moment of clarity where you lay out exactly what happened or is happening. The GM must tell you if your deduction is correct and must fill in any gaps you missed with true information.

---

## Sorcery
*Level 1: Cast limited-scope spells, safer than Wizardry. Start with three spells.*

**Level 2:** Once per day, cast a completely improvised Sorcery spell by choosing options from each list on the spot in real time.

**Level 3:** You no longer expend Sorcery spells when you cast them — your mastery means they replenish naturally. Once per day, you can cast two Sorcery spells simultaneously, combining their effects into a single action.

---

## Strategy
*Level 1: In combat, predict enemy movements, positions, and strengths.*

**Level 2:** When you read an enemy's strategy, one ally gains a bonus to their next roll against that enemy. A word relays the benefit to the whole group.

**Level 3:** Once per combat, you can call a Tactical Pause — declaring a formation or approach that grants all allies +1 to all rolls for the next full round. This requires only a word or gesture; your allies have trained to recognize your signals instantly.

---

## Summon
*Level 1: Call forth demons for counsel or binding. Start with two bound demons.*

**Level 2:** When a Bound demon performs its daily deed, you may request two actions instead of one. The demon names a price for the second before performing it.

**Level 3:** You no longer need any of the standard summoning requirements — you can call a Bound demon with a single spoken word, anywhere, at any time. Additionally, you may now attempt to bind a demon you've never met before, during an active encounter, using only your WIS and whatever leverage the situation provides.

---

## Surgeon
*Level 1: With the Heal skill, mix new medicines or bring someone back from the brink of death.*

**Level 2:** When you bring someone back from the brink, they recover with one extra Hit Die rolled and added immediately. Your medicines counter magical diseases and curses.

**Level 3:** Your healing is miraculous. You can restore a dead character to life if they died within the last hour, given ten minutes of work and basic medical supplies. They return with 1 HP and one permanent scar — the GM describes it. This can be done once per character, ever.

---

## Talented
*Level 1: Add +1 Skill.*

**Level 2:** Once per session, temporarily use a Skill you don't possess for one roll.

**Level 3:** Choose one Skill you don't possess — you now have it permanently. Additionally, you can use your once-per-session improvised skill use twice per session instead of once.

---

## Tinker
*Level 1: Pick locks and disable traps quickly and unnoticed, even in plain sight.*

**Level 2:** When you examine a lock or trap, you understand how it was built and who built it. You can re-arm any trap you've disabled, with modifications.

**Level 3:** No mundane lock or trap can stop you — you automatically succeed at disabling any non-magical mechanical device. Against magical locks and traps, you still roll, but you have advantage. You can also build a perfect functional replica of any mechanism you have examined.

---

## Tough
*Level 1: Add +1 armor.*

**Level 2:** Once per combat, completely ignore one source of damage, decided after seeing the damage roll.

**Level 3:** You reduce all incoming damage by 1 (minimum 0, applied after armor). Your once-per-combat damage immunity can now be used twice per combat. Combined with Level 1, you have +2 total armor from Tough alone.

---

## Tracker
*Level 1: Identify and follow tracks quickly while on the move.*

**Level 2:** When you follow a trail, the GM tells you the quarry's destination if they have one. You always sense when a trail has been deliberately laid to mislead you.

**Level 3:** You can track anything — not just physical creatures, but magical entities, spirits, and abstract sources: "whoever sent this message," "the origin of this curse," "whatever passed through here last." If a trail exists in any form, you can follow it.

---

## Trick
*Level 1: Distract or delay an opponent with your antics.*

**Level 2:** When you successfully Trick a target, all nearby enemies who witnessed it hesitate. Your allies gain a bonus to their next action against any of those enemies.

**Level 3:** Your tricks are so elaborate that the confusion lasts the full scene, not just a moment. Once per session, you can perform a Grand Trick — a deception so complete it changes the flow of an entire encounter: disguising an attack as an accident, making enemies fight each other, creating a distraction that persists beyond your direct involvement.

---

## Turn
*Level 1: Attempt to hold undead at bay with faith and a holy symbol.*

**Level 2:** Undead you Turn flee from you for the rest of the scene. Against powerful undead, Turn forces them to act defensively.

**Level 3:** Lesser undead (non-named, non-boss) are destroyed outright when you Turn them — not merely fled. Against powerful undead, Turn causes ongoing pain — they take d6 damage per round they remain near you, and the damage is not reduced by any form of undead resistance.

---

## Vehemence
*Level 1: When using damaging magic effects, add +1 die to damage and discard the lowest.*

**Level 2:** When you roll maximum damage on a Vehemence-enhanced spell, the target is knocked back or prone.

**Level 3:** Your magical damage ignores 2 points of magical resistance or protection. When a Vehemence-enhanced spell deals maximum damage and triggers the knockback effect (Level 2), the knockback also affects any creature adjacent to the primary target — the raw force radiates outward.

## Vision
*Level 1: Drink holy water to commune with your deity for divine guidance.*

**Level 2:** You no longer need holy water — ten minutes of meditation suffices. Once per session, your deity sends you a brief, unsolicited warning before significant danger.

**Level 3:** Your communion with your deity is continuous — a low, constant connection. Once per session, your deity may intervene directly in a small but tangible way: redirecting a blow, providing a sudden inspiration, or delivering a physical sign visible to everyone present. The GM decides the form, but it should be unambiguous.

---

## Volley
*Level 1: Add +2 damage to any ranged attack.*

**Level 2:** Once per combat, loose a volley targeting two adjacent enemies simultaneously, rolling damage separately for each.

**Level 3:** You can fire into melee without any risk of hitting allies — your precision is absolute. Your ranged attacks are so controlled that you choose exactly where each shot lands. Once per combat, you can fire at three different targets simultaneously, rolling damage separately for each.

---

## Warded
*Level 1: While using magic, protective wards grant you +1 armor.*

**Level 2:** Your wards persist for one round after you stop using magic. You can extend your ward to one nearby ally as a quick action, granting them +1 armor until your next turn.

**Level 3:** Your wards are now permanent and passive — you always have +1 armor even when not using magic. The ward you share with allies (Level 2) now lasts for the full scene instead of until your next turn.

---

## Wild
*Level 1: Converse with and attempt to command animals.*

**Level 2:** Animals you have commanded remain loyal to you even after the command expires — they will not act against you. You can command animals up to large size without additional difficulty.

**Level 3:** You can attempt to command any creature — not just animals, but monsters, summoned entities, and magical beasts. Truly intelligent creatures resist with WIS, but the attempt is always valid. Animals you've previously commanded will seek you out again if circumstances allow — your bond with the natural world is absolute.

---

## Wizardry
*Level 1: Create magical effects from raw Chaos. Power score equals base Hit Dice + WIS.*

**Level 2:** When you roll a Critical Success on a Wizardry spell, recover 1 spent Power. You may spend Power at a 2:1 ratio to extend spell duration by one rank without triggering Warp exposure for that adjustment.

**Level 3:** You no longer risk Warp exposure on Partial Successes — only a Miss exposes you to the Warp. Your Power score maximum increases by +3 permanently. When you roll a Critical Success, recover 2 Power instead of 1.

---

## WTF?
*Level 1: Bonus to roll against opponents who haven't fought you before. Lost after first confrontation.*

**Level 2:** Once per combat against any foe — even a known one — you may do something so unexpected they lose their next action processing it.

**Level 3:** Your chaos is a philosophy, not just a tactic. Once per session, you can declare that the current situation "doesn't make sense" — and the GM must introduce one genuinely unexpected element that disrupts the scene in your favor. Your enemies can never fully model your behavior; even those who know you well always treat you as an unknown quantity.
