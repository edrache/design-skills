# Core Reigns Interaction

The game should closely mimic the interaction style of Reigns.

Each turn consists of a single card displayed in the center of the screen.

The card contains:

- Illustration
- Situation description
- Two possible responses

The player does not immediately see the full text of the responses.

Instead, the player interacts by dragging the card left or right.

---

# Swipe Preview System

When the player begins dragging the card:

## Drag Left

A label gradually appears on the left side of the screen.

Example:

"Allow it"

or

"Listen patiently"

or

"Improvise"

The further the card is dragged, the more visible this choice becomes.

---

## Drag Right

A label gradually appears on the right side of the screen.

Example:

"Only one dragon"

or

"Return to the game"

or

"Force them back"

The further the card is dragged, the more visible this choice becomes.

---

# Decision Feedback

Before the card is released, the player should already understand the narrative consequence of the choice.

Example:

Card:

"A child wants to own 17 wolves."

Dragging left reveals:

"Allow it"

Dragging right reveals:

"Only one wolf"

This allows the player to make a roleplaying decision instead of a purely mechanical one.

---

# Resolution

When the card is released beyond a threshold:

- The decision is confirmed.
- Resource values are updated.
- A short feedback message appears.
- The next card is shown.

---

# Visual Resource Feedback

After every decision:

- Resource bars animate smoothly.
- Numbers briefly appear (+10, -5, etc.).
- Changes are visible but not overly prominent.

The focus should remain on the story and humor.

---

# Design Philosophy

Players should not feel like they are optimizing numbers.

Players should feel like they are making funny Game Master decisions.

The resource system exists only to create tension between:

- keeping children engaged
- protecting Paweł's remaining sanity

The best decisions should not always be obvious.

Sometimes preserving sanity will hurt engagement.

Sometimes entertaining the children will create chaos.

This tension is the core of the game.
